## Hiring Process

Our hiring process is designed to find the best talent for our team. Here's an overview of the process:

1. **Application**: Submit your application through our website.
2. **Initial Screening**: Our HR team will review your application and schedule a call to discuss your qualifications.
3. **Technical Assessment**: If you progress to the next stage, you'll complete a technical assessment to evaluate your skills.
4. **Interviews**: You'll have a series of interviews with our team to discuss your experience and fit for the role.
5. **Reference Checks**: We'll contact your previous employers to verify your experience and skills.
