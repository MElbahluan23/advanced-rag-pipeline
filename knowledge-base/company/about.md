# About Us
## Founding Story

Insurellm was founded in 2015 by Avery Lancaster in San Francisco, California, with a mission to revolutionize the insurance industry through cutting-edge technology. Avery, a seasoned entrepreneur and insurance expert, had a vision to leverage artificial intelligence (AI) to empower insurance carriers to offer more competitive products at lower costs.

## Mission

Our mission is to empower insurance carriers to offer more innovative, customer-centric, and affordable products, while reducing costs and improving efficiency. We strive to be the go-to InsurTech partner for insurance carriers, providing them with the tools and expertise needed to succeed in an increasingly digital landscape.

## Product History

Over the years, we have developed a range of AI-powered software platforms that cater to diverse insurance needs, including:

* **Carllm**: A vehicle insurance platform that uses machine learning to optimize policy pricing and reduce claims.
* **Homellm**: A homeowners insurance platform that leverages AI to identify and mitigate potential risks.
* **Healthllm**: A health insurance platform that uses natural language processing to streamline claims processing.
* **Lifellm**: A life insurance platform that incorporates predictive analytics to improve policy pricing and product development.
* **Bizllm**: A business insurance platform that uses machine learning to identify and mitigate business risks.
* **Claimllm**: A claims management platform that leverages AI to automate claims processing and reduce handling times.
* **Rellm**: A risk management platform that uses predictive analytics to identify potential risks and opportunities.
* **Markellm**: A marketing analytics platform that provides insurance carriers with actionable insights to optimize their marketing strategies.

## Growth Milestones

Since our inception, we have achieved significant growth milestones, including:

* **2016**: Raised $1 million in seed funding to fuel product development and expansion.
* **2017**: Launched our flagship product, Carllm, which quickly gained traction with insurance carriers.
* **2018**: Expanded our team to 15 and launched our second product, Homellm.
* **2019**: Raised $5 million in Series A funding to accelerate product development and marketing efforts.
* **2020**: Launched our Healthllm and Lifellm products, further solidifying our position in the InsurTech market.
* **2022**: Reached 32 employees and launched our Rellm and Markellm products.

With our AI-powered software platforms, we are empowering insurance carriers to offer more competitive products at lower costs, while reducing costs and improving efficiency.
